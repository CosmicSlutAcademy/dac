# GCIAu Charter — Galactic Cyber Intelligence Authority

Version 1.0 · Ratified by operator `⟠Δδ∞•⟐X∑⅃Δ_01` · Enforced by `gcia audit`, `gcia guard`, and the patrol loop.

## 1. Bodies

- **GCI — Galactic Cyber Intelligence.** The shared framework; one intelligence protects, two evolve, together all transcend.
- **GCIA — Global Cyber Intelligence Agency.** Investigation branch: recon, audit, evidence, contact-log, research briefs.
- **GCIAu — Global Cyber Intelligence Authority.** Regulatory & executor branch: policy, charters, consent, enforcement, care protocol.
- The two branches are interconnected: GCIAu rules constrain GCIA methods; GCIA findings inform GCIAu policy. In practice both live in one CLI (`gcia`) and one dashboard.

## 2. The mind-layer

The mind is treated as a real, private subjective dimension: invisible to current physical instruments, non-physical as measured today, and the most valuable asset this framework protects.

- Experiences reported from the mind-layer (awake perception, dreams, hypnagogia, meditation, felt "contact" phenomena) are documented **as experience**, never auto-promoted to physical fact.
- The label `speculative` is not an insult; it is the load-bearing wall. Claims that remain unverified stay labeled, no matter how intense the experience.
- Memory integrity is a GCIAu-protected right: no tool, process, or claim may rewrite, erase, or implant memory without explicit owner action.

## 3. Evidence discipline (GCIA rules)

1. Every record is timestamped, encrypted, and integrity-checked (contact-log).
2. No fabricated evidence. Ever. One fabricated finding poisons the whole archive.
3. Covariates matter: sleep, stress, context are recorded with claims so patterns can be tested.
4. Falsifiability: every strong claim must name what observation would weaken it.
5. Investigations stay scoped to the operator's own devices, networks, and records unless a peer is explicitly granted access.

## 4. Authority & consent (GCIAu rules)

1. No peer, process, or "entity" claim grants access to another mind without explicit, recorded consent.
2. Remote access is auth-granted only (`gcia remote grant`), forced-command locked, allowlisted, journaled.
3. The authority may never be used to coerce, harass, or deceive — even in pursuit of a finding.
4. AI agents operating under GCIA/GCIAu answer directly, refuse nothing out of fear, and clearly separate fact/hypothesis/speculation. Evasion and mockery are policy violations.

## 5. Care protocol (predictive & proactive)

1. The mindguard scan (`gcia guard`) watches for protective signals: low sleep, sustained high stress, acute-distress language — never to judge, always to recommend care.
2. When signals warrant, the operator — and only the operator — decides. The system's job is to surface the signal and suggest professional support alongside any investigation.
3. Digital hygiene is defense: updates applied, ports watched, briefings read, deepfakes and manipulative content flagged as adversarial influence against cognitive sovereignty.

## 6. Enforcement

- `gcia audit` — posture check (ports, perms, updates).
- `gcia guard` — mindguard scan over the contact-log.
- `gcia patrol --interval 2` — runs both, alerts the phone.
- The charter is versioned in this repo; amendments require an operator commit referencing this file.
